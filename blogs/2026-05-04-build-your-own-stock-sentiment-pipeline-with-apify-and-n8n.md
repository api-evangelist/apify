If you’re tracking stock sentiment manually, you’re probably stuck in one of two modes. You’re either checking social media and finance sites yourself, searching for ticker mentions and reading posts one by one, or you’re using a closed sentiment platform that gives you prebuilt dashboards, but little control over the data sources, search terms, or how often the data is collected.There’s a third option, though: build your own stock sentiment analysis pipeline.

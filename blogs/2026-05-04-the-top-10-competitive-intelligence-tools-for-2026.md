The competitive intelligence tool landscape is broader than well-known names like Semrush, Klue, or Crayon. It also includes lesser-known tools and flexible DIY solutions like Apify and Dify that let you build custom workflows instead of relying on out-of-the-box software.Not all tools achieve their goal in the same way. Some focus on sales enablement, others on marketing and market insights, SEO and AI visibility, AI-assisted analysis, or even visual website change tracking.This guide breaks down the best competitive intelligence tools.

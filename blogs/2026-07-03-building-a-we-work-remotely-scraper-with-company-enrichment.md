An Apify Actor tracks remote job openings by scraping We Work Remotely listings with CheerioCrawler, using three labeled request handlers for categories, job details, and company enrichment. The build handles ad-listing filtering, dynamic CSS classes, duplicate-crawl prevention, and free vs. paid usage limits, merging job records with company HQ, industry, and website data.

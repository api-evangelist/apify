AI agents that run paid ads need to know what the competition is doing. Adside built AdsSpy to pull any competitor's live ads from the Meta, Google, and LinkedIn ad libraries into one view, then pointed the same pipeline at all 6,055 Y Combinator companies in one overnight run.

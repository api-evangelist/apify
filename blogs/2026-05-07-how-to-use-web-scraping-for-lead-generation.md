The purpose of generating leads is to make contact with people who are potentially interested in what you have to offer. Get them to warm up to you, and they could become qualified prospects willing to purchase your service or product. That means more revenue. But if you spend ages manually looking for leads that have no potential, you’re wasting your time. Many people simply don’t realize there are better and faster ways to find leads. And one of the most efficient ways is lead scraping.
